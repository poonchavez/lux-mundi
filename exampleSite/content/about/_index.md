Lux Mundi is a cinematic Hugo theme for independent filmmakers, production studios, and creative teams. It gives your work a clear, considered home, with a dark visual palette, warm gold accents, and editorial typography.

## Made to showcase the work

The homepage introduces your studio and brings selected projects to the foreground. Visitors can continue to a dedicated portfolio, browse project cards, and open individual pages for more detail. Add a title, subtitle, year, category, poster or still, and an optional external link to give each project its own context.

## A visual language with room to breathe

The theme pairs a deep, film-inspired background with warm gold accents, expressive serif headings, and clean sans-serif body text. Generous spacing keeps the focus on your images and writing. Layouts adapt to mobile and desktop screens, while semantic page structure, a skip link, and reduced-motion support help make the site more accessible.

## Make it your own

Set your studio name, homepage headline, introduction, and navigation in Hugo's configuration file. Add pages and projects through regular Markdown content files, and keep project images alongside their page or in the site's `static` directory. The theme uses Hugo templates and CSS, with no JavaScript dependencies, so the site remains easy to understand and extend.

## Start with the example site

The bundled example includes a working configuration, About and project pages, and Journal tutorials. Use it to see how the theme fits together, then replace its sample content with your own. If you are new to Hugo, the Journal section walks through installation, project pages, homepage settings, and publishing.

Lux Mundi is designed as a starting point rather than a fixed format: keep the structure that serves your work, adjust the copy and navigation, and build out the pages your audience needs.
