Lux Mundi is an independent production studio drawn to stories that open a window into another life. We work with filmmakers and creative partners to bring thoughtful, human-centred projects from first idea to final frame.

## Light, held in common

We believe a good story can make the unfamiliar feel close. Our work is guided by curiosity, care, and a respect for the people whose experiences appear on screen.
